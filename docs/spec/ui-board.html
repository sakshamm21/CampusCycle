Pass motion
  • 01Tilt and shine. The card follows the phone's tilt and a light streak crosses the finish.
  • 02Mint. Approval reveals the card left to right with a sweep and a haptic tap.
  • 03Dock. After slide to borrow, the bike number drops into the chip slot with a thunk.
  • 04Glow. While riding the card breathes in the accent; amber when overdue.
  • 05Stamp. Returned lands with a bounce, then the pass is free again.
Reanimated · Skia · expo-sensors · expo-haptics · CSS 3D on web
Sync DNA
  • Heavy lowercase headlines with one italic serif word in the accent colour
  • Tiny mono uppercase labels for metadata
  • Tilted stickers, a pulsing live dot and the marquee bands
  • Big rounded cards and pill buttons that squish when tapped
  • A floating dock with a raised centre button, now Borrow
  • Scoreboard numbers on the admin dashboard
  • Dark-first look with its own day theme, soft glows and film grain
The Rider Pass layer
  • No lock screen and no codes anywhere
  • The Rider Pass as the hero, with CRED-style tilt, flip, mint, dock and stamp
  • Finishes that level up with rides: Graphite, Chrome, Holo
  • A live pass QR on the back that refreshes every 30 seconds
  • Slide to borrow, and a three-tick return: at a stand, same bike, photo
  • Admin pass scan and stand check
