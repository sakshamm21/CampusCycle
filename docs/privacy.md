# Privacy Policy · CampusCycle

Last updated: October 2026

CampusCycle is a bicycle pool platform designed for university campuses. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about what we collect, why we need it, and how long we keep it.

---

## 1. What We Collect

When you use CampusCycle, we collect only the minimum information required to operate a reliable and accountable bicycle sharing fleet:

- **Account details:** Your full name, university email address, phone number, and student roll or employee ID number.
- **Campus ID photo:** Optional photo of your campus identification card, uploaded solely for administrator verification.
- **Location data:** Your device's GPS coordinates are read **only** at the moment you scan to borrow a bike and scan to return a bike, to verify that you are inside a designated stand geofence. **There is no background GPS tracking or route tracking during your ride.**
- **Ride history:** Timestamps of when rides start and finish, origin stand, destination stand, duration, and assigned bicycle code.
- **Photos:** Photographs of bicycles parked at return stands, and any photos you attach when submitting a maintenance issue report.
- **TORQ conversations:** Transcripts of text and voice interactions with TORQ, our in-app AI assistant, along with ephemeral memories stored to improve subsequent interactions.
- **Device tokens:** Push notification registration tokens for critical alerts (approvals, ride time reminders, and overdue notices).

---

## 2. Why We Collect It

We collect this information strictly for the following operational purposes:

- Verifying campus eligibility and preventing unauthorized access to college-owned assets.
- Ensuring bicycles are borrowed and returned only at designated stands with proper parking proof.
- Alerting riders before rides exceed college duration limits.
- Assisting administrators in fleet maintenance, balancing, and dispute resolution.
- Enabling TORQ to answer rider questions, check return photos for proper parking, and assist fleet operations.

---

## 3. Who Sees It

- **Your College Administrators:** Authorized administrators of your specific college can view your name, email, roll number, ride history, and any reported issues.
- **Super Administrators:** Platform administrators who manage multi-campus infrastructure, audit logs, and system health.
- **AI Providers:** Interactions with TORQ and photos sent for automated visual inspection are processed via OpenRouter models. ID photos and return photos are sent exclusively to providers that **do not retain or use data for model training**.
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. There are no advertising trackers or marketing analytics tools embedded in CampusCycle.

---

## 4. How Long We Keep It

We apply strict automatic retention and cleanup policies to prevent unnecessary data storage:

- **Return photos:** Automatically deleted from storage 30 days after the ride concludes.
- **TORQ chat photos:** Automatically deleted after 30 days.
- **Issue report photos:** Automatically deleted 30 days after the associated issue report is resolved.
- **Campus ID card photos:** Stored in a private, encrypted bucket until your account is deleted.
- **Ride records:** Historical ride records are preserved for institutional reporting and fleet auditing. If your account is deleted, personal identifying information is scrubbed and the records are anonymized.

---

## 5. Your Choices and Controls

You have complete control over your data:

- **Theme & Preferences:** Configure appearance and notification preferences in your Profile.
- **Clear AI Memory:** You can clear TORQ's conversational memory at any time directly from the app.
- **Delete Account:** You can request account deletion at any time in the app (Profile → Delete Account) or by emailing the owner. Upon deletion, your active passes are cancelled, profile data is removed, and historical ride logs are anonymized.
